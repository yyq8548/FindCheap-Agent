# FindCheap Agent

This is the product summary. The maintained [Agent design](docs/architecture/agent-design.md) defines approved behavior, architecture, implementation gaps and acceptance; target rules are not claims of current capability.

FindCheap helps people find relevant, trustworthy, good-value products without buying on their behalf.

## Product promise

- Search current official stores, reviewed merchants, approved affiliate feeds, and eligible marketplaces.
- Put relevance before price, commission, or catalog source.
- Explain the strongest supported choice in the user's language.
- Preserve product and variant identity for follow-up inspection, deals, and quotes.
- Never invent identity, availability, discounts, merchant authorization, or delivered totals.
- Never check out, reserve inventory, purchase, or pay.

## Result order

Return at most eight cards: up to two official-store matches, three trusted exact or similar matches, and three high-match best-value options. Missing evidence is labeled; unrelated products are not used to fill space.
