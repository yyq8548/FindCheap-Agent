## Scope and safety

- Requested named editions require primary product fields. FAQ/cross-sell Mild text cannot promote a regular product into a Mild match. Missing evidence remains unknown; ordinary garment length and fit words remain usable.
- All derived snapshots share recommendation eligibility and final comparison/count/value reconciliation. Inspection preserves the original goal, requirements, immutable selections and history; unresolved cards cannot become trusted or value recommendations merely because their merchant is trusted.
- Recovery consent belongs to the shopping goal, while one-use leases remain bound to the exact approved render. Inspection and budget continuation cannot automatically re-prompt after a terminal denial, cancellation or unavailable form capability. Concurrent admission and the existing transient-attempt limit remain enforced.
- Final text and structured recovery status agree. The QA replay checks excluded wrong editions and research-only groups, not merely that one correct candidate exists.

No dependency, Backend rewrite, database migration, merchant trust expansion, payment permission or host approval-policy change. No live Cart writes, Watch operations or production DB integration in release verification. Native Codex immediate decline without a visible popup remains unresolved; simulated clients do not establish real native consent, Chrome continuation or delivered-price acceptance.
