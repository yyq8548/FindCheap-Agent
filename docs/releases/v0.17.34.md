## Scope and safety

- Sony headphone clarifications retain the search goal and constraints while allowing supported same-family refinement. Conflicting categories, brands and generations remain rejected.
- Merchant requirements use server-owned trust evidence, including late exact-domain Awin enrichment. Product descriptions do not verify a merchant; location and delivery remain unknown when evidence is absent.
- A selected Coupon follow-up can resolve exactly one synchronized selection from the same card view. Missing, empty, multiple, expired and foreign selections remain distinct; the target is locked before source work.
- Sony discovery filters unrelated numeric-family hits before URL validation. Safe source diagnostics identify stages and field categories without retaining raw URLs, payloads or credentials. The historic schema failure's exact field remains unproven.
- Chrome guidance checks browser inventory before the existing approval lease. Explicit unavailable, timed-out and cancelled outcomes can close their approved session without source reads, new permission or snapshot mutation. Host-owned browser startup performance is not claimed fixed.
- Search headers keep current requirements and the host's Search products label. Duplicate global coverage/count/research banners are removed; selection controls, per-card evidence and limitations, and concise empty/error states remain.

No new dependencies, database migration, payment capability, trust-policy expansion or permission bypass. Actual shopping and native post-install acceptance remain separate from automated tests.
