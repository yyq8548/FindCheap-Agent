Identity/authorization failure, broken readiness or sustained source regression blocks rollout and requires rollback/containment. Ordinary transient source failure preserves last valid Feed snapshots.

## Changes and safety

- Classify sanitized DNS/request/body transport failures separately from policy and TLS rejection. Typed transient failures can retain existing host-authorized recovery eligibility; unknown and unsafe failures stay closed.
- Retry eligible interactive candidate-image transport failure at most once, in the execution layer's original request/time/cancellation budget. Retry is not added to Watch, quotes or Feed adapters. Safe code/phase/source-host diagnostics do not expose private URLs or error bodies.
- Repair Sony official search poisoning by unrelated observations and narrowly ignore incomplete, nonselected, hidden, unavailable same-model sibling metadata. Selected model/SKU/URL/color/price/stock checks remain mandatory.
- Add the reviewed exact medicube US official host to embedded/default registries. Current managed trust is authoritative: an embedded seed never bypasses a missing managed approval. This does not verify the original requested 155g package.
- Distinguish omitted prior references, unavailable process-local state and known expiry. Correct references are not retried endlessly when their state is gone. No cross-restart shopping memory is implemented.
- Add a read-only preflight and frozen original-case replay. Protocol presence and loaded candidate images are not native-host or image-accuracy acceptance.

Nine comparison rows, source-owned prices, exact identity, immutable selection IDs, trust/quality separation, one-use quote approval, no automatic purchase/payment and bounded URL/redirect validation remain unchanged.
