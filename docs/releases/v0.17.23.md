## Changes

- Confirmed visual differences remove stale EXACT labels from candidates and the card source without inventing identity or changing merchant trust.
- Returning from comparison retains same-snapshot card choices and monotonic revisions. Delayed comparison, quote and hydration responses cannot overwrite a newer view.
- A ZIP in search or Coupon research does not implicitly create a Shopify cart. Separate explicit quote tools remain available, with their unresolved authorization and side-effect qualification tracked in the overall design.
- Restock checks require reliable same-merchant, same-source, same-variant and same-condition OUT_OF_STOCK to IN_STOCK evidence. They preserve reliable observations on unknown or failed reads, prioritize the tracked offer and reject inventory time regression.
- New Watch rules default to 30 days. Duplicate creation and restart do not renew the deadline or resume paused rules. Explicit deadlines and legacy records remain unchanged.
