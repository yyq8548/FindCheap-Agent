# FindCheap Agent v0.17.13

## Changes

- Preserve reliable visual pattern, palette and structural clues through bounded
  retrieval and review. Deduplicate raw product images across resizing budgets.
- Add privacy-safe, bounded visual funnel diagnostics and clearer incomplete,
  conflicting and insufficient-evidence outcomes.
- Add a reviewed official-catalog backend adapter with bounded independent
  category discovery, persistent refresh limits, expiry and live registry checks.
- Keep product references merchant-qualified and tied to immutable render
  snapshots. Existing cards remain the authority for follow-up comparisons.
- Correct relative official offer parsing and variant/color-specific stock
  evidence. Cards distinguish selected variants from product-color availability;
  missing prices, stock and trust have separate recommendation reason codes.
- Add image-worker packaging, cancellation and byte-budget checks, and frozen
  visual acceptance cohorts with held-out-style and human-review gates.

## Safety

Identity, merchant trust, variant selection and price remain server-owned and
fail closed. Visual similarity does not establish exact identity. Commission
does not determine relevance. The agent remains read-only: no purchase, payment,
checkout or inventory reservation.

Private photos, PDFs, captures and credentials are excluded from the release.
Official-catalog files are runtime state, not bundled answer fixtures.

## Known limitations

The six-image development trial completed 12/12 runs with 72 image inspections,
but target recall remains **0/6 image-only and 1/6 with brand**. This is an
engineering/reliability release, not a claim that visual-search accuracy targets
have passed. The 40-sample acceptance gate remains outstanding. Registry
expansion and broader catalog coverage are separate follow-up work.

The preceding worktree trial and exact bundle digest are retained in
[the verification report](../testing/visual-search-six-sample-remediation-results.md).
Its unreleased status describes that earlier checkpoint, not this release.
