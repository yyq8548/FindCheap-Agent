# FindCheap Agent v0.18.5

Repairs defects observed in native shopping task `01a0874b-551b-7e20-bda2-6ae43179799a`. This release does not declare the deferred image corpus, full Watch delivery lifecycle, or independent business acceptance complete.

## Changes

- Recognize selected `auto-drip`, `french`, and `home-espresso` grinds. Ground variants cannot satisfy whole-bean or capsule requests through a shared parent title.
- Apply the same selected coffee-form and machine-system evidence to required features, exclusions and preferences. Description-only compatibility remains unverified. Each exact system requirement is assessed independently; an unparsed combined condition stays unknown rather than borrowing one branch's result.
- Exclude a capsule whose unambiguous, current-product description explicitly limits it to another single machine system. This negative-only check cannot establish compatibility or override a selected variant; ordinary marketing, other products and multiple systems remain unverified. The native Peet's Midtown L’OR-only counterexample is retained as a regression.
- `CORRECT_PREVIOUS_PRODUCT` and `CONTINUE_PREVIOUS_PRODUCT` both support explicit, snapshot-bound removal of named prior requirements. Budget and unrelated requirements remain; unbound or fabricated withdrawals are rejected for text and visual tools.
- Sony's missing `notSellable` no longer discards an otherwise valid product identity and item price. Missing sellability remains unknown; invalid values, URLs and variant identity still fail closed.
- Woo subscription identities without a verified billing scope retain raw source amounts and stock observations, but do not become verified item-price cards or price-watch targets. Normal zero-price items remain supported. An explicit one-time purchase in the current product name can disambiguate a general subscription category, but not a directly named subscription product.
