# FindCheap Agent v0.17.21

## Authorized scope

Decision: **LIMITED GO**. Chris requested commit, push, deployment to the existing
Railway production service, and replacement of the installed Codex plugin after
receiving the outstanding business and real-host acceptance limitations.
This is a reliability and precision release, not a declaration of complete
shopping coverage or verified desktop web-recovery acceptance.

## Improvements

- Preserve stated shopping symptoms: reducing dryness/frizz is distinct from
  suitability for dry hair. Reviewed functional uses in `primaryUse` are necessary
  conditions; unknown free-text preferences are not automatically hardened.
- Refine product identity within the same goal without losing budget, use or
  selected snapshot provenance. Generic shorthand cannot erase a known identity;
  another model or character requires an explicit correction or new goal.
- Apply role, franchise and default-appearance evidence to the same product.
  Query-only appearance refinement works without duplicate requiredFeatures;
  unrelated everyday wigs cannot fill named-character research results.
- Make explicit cosplay use a required fit condition. Reject clear quote-fee or
  deposit-only listings as complete-item price offers; ordinary full-price custom
  products and general deposit policies are not automatically excluded.
- Ask one front-loaded EV clarification per goal, then permit bounded discovery.
  Unknown region, vehicle/connector and complete-charger requirements still block
  purchase recommendations and survive reassessment.
- Compile bounded source queries separately from final identity/eligibility;
  retain server-issued goal revisions and revalidate previous candidates.
- Share scoped merchant-claim, requirement and comparable-value evidence across
  cards and comparisons. Keep the server primary in its display group, preserve
  ordinal references, and avoid raw-price savings claims across unlike products.
- Expose typed source failures and unique-candidate funnel diagnostics. Offline
  business evaluation keeps frozen evidence, human labels and real-host gates
  separate from known development examples.

## Safety and non-goals

All existing execution/network, merchant trust, immutable selection, URL/variant/
price binding and consent boundaries remain. Source text is untrusted evidence,
not authority. Claims about ingredients, another product, conditional use or
negated benefits cannot establish the requested product's function. Merchant
claims are not independent efficacy or quality verification.

No automatic merchant approval, wider redirect policy, increased search budget,
local product catalog, new provider, checkout, inventory reservation, purchase or
payment is introduced. Private traces and raw development captures remain ignored
local artifacts, not release contents.
