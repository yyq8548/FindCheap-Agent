## Scope and safety

- Requirement-matched products with valid high product ratings can appear in tier two. Their badge says high-rated product, not independently verified merchant. This does not grant a primary recommendation, best-value status or Cart permission. Merchant-rating admission still needs its own trustworthy source and threshold.
- Leads without a verified item price do not become new cards. Final card counts, comparison facts, public prose and model-visible recovery counts agree after inspection. Source observations and historical snapshots remain intact; missing shipping or tax alone does not remove a priced card.
- A unique selection synchronized to the original card view can be inspected directly. Missing, empty, multiple, expired and foreign selections remain distinct. Sequential inspections explicitly chain derived snapshots; parallel branches are not silently merged.
- Shopping guidance uses concise, factual language, respects required host instructions and avoids repeating progress. The plugin introduction no longer embeds a release-history wall of text. Selected-product inspection removes complete style/script blocks from description evidence without claiming general HTML sanitization.

No new dependencies, database migration, trust-registry mutation, payment capability or permission bypass. Native popup recovery is a separate host task after this release. This package does not claim that the host popup, actual merchant quotes, independent image accuracy or the whole Agent design has passed acceptance.
