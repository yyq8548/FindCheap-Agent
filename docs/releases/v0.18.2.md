# FindCheap v0.18.2

Coffee search repair for the reported “比较咖啡产品” task. The original continuation failed before searching, and the restarted request returned no products. Current verification and delivery states are recorded separately below.

The repair addresses three failures in the reported coffee task:

1. A clarification from coffee to whole-bean coffee no longer produces a false product-context conflict. Controlled refinements to ground coffee, pods and instant coffee also retain the original goal, budget and other requirements. Reviewed category-label equivalents are accepted; exact product identity, conflicting forms and unverified identity-query translations retain their existing checks.
2. Controlled category-only Woo queries use a useful coffee retrieval term within the existing bounded search. Equivalent category words are not duplicated into a restrictive query such as `coffee beans whole bean coffee`. The original requirements remain available for candidate validation.
3. Coffee candidate assessment distinguishes the actual product and selected form from coffee-themed merchandise, equipment, unrelated food and unselected parent options. Missing form evidence remains unresolved. A selected ground-coffee offer cannot satisfy a whole-bean request; merchant trust and USD pricing do not prove US delivery.

Shopify selected-product inspection also reuses the coffee assessment before creating a derived snapshot. Unknown form stays unverified, a confirmed conflicting grind is excluded, and a confirmed whole-bean variant can become eligible. Old snapshots remain intact.

The [research, frozen plan and verification record](../engineering/changes/2026-09-08-coffee-search-repair.md) preserves the original failure denominator and separates the pre-search context error from the later retrieval failure. It does not attribute the latter to an omitted authorized browser fallback.

The 200-store access registry, the Woo review dispositions of 137 official stores / 45 established retailers / 18 search-only stores, and the published trust database are unchanged by this patch. No merchant promotion, new source quota, extra search pass or database migration is included. Exact Woo anchors, selected products, immutable snapshots, read-only source access and the existing permission boundaries remain in force.

Product-card resources advance to `v43`; comparison resources advance to `v13` because both embed the runtime version. The plugin manifest uses a new UTC cache identifier for this source package.
